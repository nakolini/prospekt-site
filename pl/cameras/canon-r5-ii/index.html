Portrait distances on the Canon R5 II

How far to stand from the subject with the Canon R5 II held vertically, so the subject fills 90% of the frame height. Full body means a 1.8 m person, head and shoulders about 0.5 m.

  • With 35 mm on the Canon R5 II: about 1,9 m (6,4 ft) for a full-body portrait, 0,5 m (1,8 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 50 mm on the Canon R5 II: about 2,8 m (9,1 ft) for a full-body portrait, 0,8 m (2,5 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 85 mm on the Canon R5 II: about 4,7 m (15 ft) for a full-body portrait, 1,3 m (4,3 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 135 mm on the Canon R5 II: about 7,5 m (25 ft) for a full-body portrait, 2,1 m (6,8 ft) for head and shoulders.

Pytania

Jak wygląda obiektyw 50 mm na aparacie Canon R5 II?

Kadruje jak 50 mm na pełnej klatce, z poziomym kątem widzenia 39,6°.

Jaki obiektyw da na aparacie Canon R5 II kadr 50 mm z pełnej klatki?

Obiektyw o ogniskowej około 50 mm. Kadruje jak 50 mm na pełnej klatce.

How far do I stand for a full-body portrait with an 85 mm lens on the Canon R5 II?

About 4,7 m (15 ft) from the subject, with the camera held vertically. That fits a 1.8 m person in 90% of the frame height. For head and shoulders, stand about 1,3 m (4,3 ft) away.

How much does a 35 mm lens cover at 3 m on the Canon R5 II?

About 3,1 × 2,1 m, width by height, with the camera held horizontally. At 10 m it covers about 10,3 × 6,9 m.
