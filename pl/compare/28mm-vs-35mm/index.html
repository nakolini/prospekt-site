28 mm vs 35 mm: which should you choose?

28 mm is a little wider and suits tight streets, crowds and small rooms. 35 mm is a little tighter and suits people, travel and everyday photos, with less stretching at the edges. On a full-frame camera, 28 mm sees 65,5° across and covers 3,9 × 2,6 m at 3 m. 35 mm sees 54,4° and covers 3,1 × 2,1 m. From the same spot, 35 mm makes everything 1,25x bigger.

How 28 mm and 35 mm frame the same scene

Which one to choose

  • The difference is small. From the same spot, 28 mm shows about 25% more width than 35 mm: 3,9 × 2,6 m against 3,1 × 2,1 m at 3 m.
  • Pick 28 mm for street photography close to people, in crowds and narrow streets, and for small rooms.
  • Pick 35 mm if you also take portraits or photograph food and details. It's easier to fill the frame with one subject.
  • Fixed-lens cameras split the same way: the Ricoh GR and the Leica Q3 are 28 mm, and the Fujifilm X100 series frames like 35 mm.

Compare them over a photo

Pytania

Should I get a 28 mm or a 35 mm lens?

28 mm is a little wider and suits tight streets, crowds and small rooms. 35 mm is a little tighter and suits people, travel and everyday photos, with less stretching at the edges. On full frame, 28 mm covers 3,9 × 2,6 m at 3 m and 35 mm covers 3,1 × 2,1 m. Compare both over a photo in the free lens comparison tool.

How far do I stand for a full-body portrait with 28 mm or 35 mm?

On full frame, with the camera held vertically, about 1,6 m (5,1 ft) with 28 mm and 1,9 m (6,4 ft) with 35 mm. That fits a 1.8 m person in 90% of the frame height. On 1.5x APS-C, about 2,3 m (7,7 ft) and 2,9 m (9,6 ft).

What do 28 mm and 35 mm frame like on APS-C?

On a 1.5x APS-C camera, 28 mm frames like 42 mm on full frame and 35 mm like 52 mm. On Canon APS-C (1.6x), like 45 mm and 56 mm. For the full-frame look on 1.5x APS-C, use about 18 mm and 23 mm.
