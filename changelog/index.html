Changelog

Every Prospekt release, newest first. The same notes appear in the App Store.

1.3.2 October 3, 2026

  • New About section in Settings: rate Prospekt, copy our support email, see the app version.
  • Fixed: the shutter could stop responding after a failed capture.

1.3.1 April 27, 2026

  • Translation and performance improvements.

1.3 April 2026

  • Custom cameras: add your own camera body with any crop factor.

1.2 April 2026

  • Extender (teleconverter) support: 1.4x and 2x.

1.1 March 2026

  • Added the Canon EOS 6D, 6D Mark II and 7D Mark II.
  • Moved the focal length wheel to the right side for easier one-handed use.
  • Improved the landscape layout.

1.0 March 2026

  • First release: a live viewfinder that shows what a 14 to 300 mm lens frames on your camera body.
