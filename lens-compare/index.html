How far to stand for a portrait

On a full-frame camera held vertically, with the subject filling 90% of the frame height. Full body means a 1.8 m person, head and shoulders about 0.5 m.

  • 35 mm: about 1.9 m (6.4 ft) for a full-body portrait, 0.5 m (1.8 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • 50 mm: about 2.8 m (9.1 ft) for a full-body portrait, 0.8 m (2.5 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • 85 mm: about 4.7 m (15 ft) for a full-body portrait, 1.3 m (4.3 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • 135 mm: about 7.5 m (25 ft) for a full-body portrait, 2.1 m (6.8 ft) for head and shoulders.

How the frames are calculated

  • A lens frames like its focal length times the crop factor on full frame. That number is the full-frame equivalent.
  • The angle of view is 2 x arctan(sensor size / (2 x focal length)), for the width, the height or the diagonal of the sensor.
  • Frames are drawn on a flat (rectilinear) projection, which is how all normal lenses draw. Fisheye lenses are different.
  • Micro Four Thirds and 44 x 33 medium format sensors are 4:3, so their frames are taller than the 3:2 frames of full frame and APS-C.
  • Coverage at a distance is the distance x sensor size / focal length. It assumes the lens is focused far away. Close up, most lenses frame a little tighter.

Questions

How do I compare two lenses without buying them?

Pick your camera above and tap both focal lengths. The frames show what each one captures from the same spot. To see it on a real scene, Prospekt does the same live through your iPhone.

Is 35 mm or 50 mm better for everyday photos?

35 mm shows more of the scene and suits streets, travel and small rooms. 50 mm is tighter and suits portraits and details. Compare both frames on your camera above and pick the one closer to how you see.

Does the tool work on a phone?

Yes. It runs in any modern browser, and your own photos are processed on your device.

How far away do I stand for a full-body portrait?

On a full-frame camera held vertically, about 1.9 m (6.4 ft) with a 35 mm lens, 2.8 m (9.1 ft) with 50 mm, 4.7 m (15 ft) with 85 mm and 7.5 m (25 ft) with 135 mm. That fits a 1.8 m person in 90% of the frame height. Every camera page lists these distances for that body.

How far do I stand for a full-body portrait with an 85 mm lens on APS-C?

About 7.1 m (23 ft) on a 1.5x APS-C camera such as Sony, Fujifilm or Nikon, and 7.6 m (25 ft) on Canon APS-C (1.6x), with the camera held vertically and a 1.8 m person filling 90% of the frame height. Held horizontally, about 10.6 m (35 ft) on 1.5x. With a 50 mm lens on 1.5x APS-C, about 4.2 m (14 ft).
