Portrait distances on the Canon R50

How far to stand from the subject with the Canon R50 held vertically, so the subject fills 90% of the frame height. Full body means a 1.8 m person, head and shoulders about 0.5 m.

  • With 35 mm on the Canon R50: about 3,1 m (10 ft) for a full-body portrait, 0,9 m (2,8 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 50 mm on the Canon R50: about 4,4 m (15 ft) for a full-body portrait, 1,2 m (4,1 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 85 mm on the Canon R50: about 7,6 m (25 ft) for a full-body portrait, 2,1 m (6,9 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 135 mm on the Canon R50: about 12,0 m (39 ft) for a full-body portrait, 3,3 m (11 ft) for head and shoulders.

Fragen

Wie sieht ein 50-mm-Objektiv an der Canon R50 aus?

Es wirkt wie 80 mm an Vollformat, mit einem horizontalen Bildwinkel von 25,4°.

Welches Objektiv ergibt an der Canon R50 den Look von 50 mm an Vollformat?

Ein Objektiv mit etwa 31 mm. Es wirkt wie 50 mm an Vollformat.

How far do I stand for a full-body portrait with an 85 mm lens on the Canon R50?

About 7,6 m (25 ft) from the subject, with the camera held vertically. That fits a 1.8 m person in 90% of the frame height. For head and shoulders, stand about 2,1 m (6,9 ft) away.

How much does a 35 mm lens cover at 3 m on the Canon R50?

About 1,9 × 1,3 m, width by height, with the camera held horizontally. At 10 m it covers about 6,4 × 4,3 m.
