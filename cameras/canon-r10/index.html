Canon R10: what each focal length frames

The Canon R10 has a 1.6x crop factor (Canon APS-C sensor), so a lens frames like its focal length times 1.6x on full frame. A 50 mm lens on the Canon R10 frames like 80 mm on full frame.

Lens mount: Canon RF.

Classic full-frame looks on the Canon R10

To get the framing a full-frame shooter means by these focal lengths, use these on the Canon R10:

  • To frame like 24 mm on full frame, use 15 mm on the Canon R10.
  • To frame like 35 mm on full frame, use 22 mm on the Canon R10.
  • To frame like 50 mm on full frame, use 31 mm on the Canon R10.
  • To frame like 85 mm on full frame, use 53 mm on the Canon R10.
  • To frame like 135 mm on full frame, use 84 mm on the Canon R10.

Questions

What does a 50 mm lens look like on the Canon R10?

Which lens gives a 50 mm full-frame look on the Canon R10?
