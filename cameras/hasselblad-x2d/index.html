Hasselblad X2D: what each focal length frames

The Hasselblad X2D has a 0.79x crop factor (44 x 33 medium format sensor), so a lens frames like its focal length times 0.79x on full frame. A 50 mm lens on the Hasselblad X2D frames like 40 mm on full frame.

Lens mount: Hasselblad XCD.

To see these focal lengths live, Prospekt has the Hasselblad X2D built in: pick a focal length, point your iPhone, and the screen frames what that lens would on the Hasselblad X2D. iPhone app, $3.99 once, no subscription.

Classic full-frame looks on the Hasselblad X2D

To get the framing a full-frame shooter means by these focal lengths, use these on the Hasselblad X2D:

  • To frame like 24 mm on full frame, use 30 mm on the Hasselblad X2D.
  • To frame like 35 mm on full frame, use 44 mm on the Hasselblad X2D.
  • To frame like 50 mm on full frame, use 63 mm on the Hasselblad X2D.
  • To frame like 85 mm on full frame, use 108 mm on the Hasselblad X2D.
  • To frame like 135 mm on full frame, use 171 mm on the Hasselblad X2D.

Common zoom ranges on the Hasselblad X2D

  • A 32-64 mm zoom on the Hasselblad X2D frames like 25-51 mm on full frame, a horizontal angle of view from 68.8° to 37.8°.
  • A 45-100 mm zoom on the Hasselblad X2D frames like 36-79 mm on full frame, a horizontal angle of view from 51.9° to 24.7°.
  • A 100-200 mm zoom on the Hasselblad X2D frames like 79-158 mm on full frame, a horizontal angle of view from 24.7° to 12.5°.

Portrait distances on the Hasselblad X2D

How far to stand from the subject with the Hasselblad X2D held vertically, so the subject fills 90% of the frame height. Full body means a 1.8 m person, head and shoulders about 0.5 m.

  • With 35 mm on the Hasselblad X2D: about 1.6 m (5.2 ft) for a full-body portrait, 0.4 m (1.5 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 50 mm on the Hasselblad X2D: about 2.3 m (7.5 ft) for a full-body portrait, 0.6 m (2.1 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 85 mm on the Hasselblad X2D: about 3.9 m (13 ft) for a full-body portrait, 1.1 m (3.5 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 135 mm on the Hasselblad X2D: about 6.2 m (20 ft) for a full-body portrait, 1.7 m (5.6 ft) for head and shoulders.

Questions

What does a 50 mm lens look like on the Hasselblad X2D?

It frames like 40 mm on full frame, with a horizontal angle of view of 47.3°.

Which lens gives a 50 mm full-frame look on the Hasselblad X2D?

A lens of about 63 mm. It frames like 50 mm on full frame.

Should I get a 35 mm or a 50 mm lens for the Hasselblad X2D?

On the Hasselblad X2D, 35 mm frames like 28 mm on full frame and covers about 3.8 × 2.8 m at 3 m; 50 mm frames like 40 mm and covers about 2.6 × 2.0 m. The wider 35 mm suits streets, travel and rooms; 50 mm is tighter and suits portraits and details. For the classic full-frame 35 mm look, use about 44 mm.

How far do I stand for a full-body portrait with an 85 mm lens on the Hasselblad X2D?

About 3.9 m (13 ft) from the subject, with the camera held vertically. That fits a 1.8 m person in 90% of the frame height. For head and shoulders, stand about 1.1 m (3.5 ft) away.

How much does a 35 mm lens cover at 3 m on the Hasselblad X2D?

About 3.8 × 2.8 m, width by height, with the camera held horizontally. At 10 m it covers about 12.5 × 9.4 m.
