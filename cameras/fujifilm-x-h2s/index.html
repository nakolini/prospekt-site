Fujifilm X-H2S: what each focal length frames

The Fujifilm X-H2S has a 1.5x crop factor (APS-C sensor), so a lens frames like its focal length times 1.5x on full frame. A 50 mm lens on the Fujifilm X-H2S frames like 75 mm on full frame.

Lens mount: Fujifilm X.

Classic full-frame looks on the Fujifilm X-H2S

To get the framing a full-frame shooter means by these focal lengths, use these on the Fujifilm X-H2S:

  • To frame like 24 mm on full frame, use 16 mm on the Fujifilm X-H2S.
  • To frame like 35 mm on full frame, use 23 mm on the Fujifilm X-H2S.
  • To frame like 50 mm on full frame, use 33 mm on the Fujifilm X-H2S.
  • To frame like 85 mm on full frame, use 57 mm on the Fujifilm X-H2S.
  • To frame like 135 mm on full frame, use 90 mm on the Fujifilm X-H2S.
