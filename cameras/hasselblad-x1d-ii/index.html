Hasselblad X1D II: what each focal length frames

The Hasselblad X1D II has a 0.79x crop factor (44 x 33 medium format sensor), so a lens frames like its focal length times 0.79x on full frame. A 50 mm lens on the Hasselblad X1D II frames like 40 mm on full frame.

Lens mount: Hasselblad XCD.

Classic full-frame looks on the Hasselblad X1D II

To get the framing a full-frame shooter means by these focal lengths, use these on the Hasselblad X1D II:

  • To frame like 24 mm on full frame, use 30 mm on the Hasselblad X1D II.
  • To frame like 35 mm on full frame, use 44 mm on the Hasselblad X1D II.
  • To frame like 50 mm on full frame, use 63 mm on the Hasselblad X1D II.
  • To frame like 85 mm on full frame, use 108 mm on the Hasselblad X1D II.
  • To frame like 135 mm on full frame, use 171 mm on the Hasselblad X1D II.
