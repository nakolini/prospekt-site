Portrait distances on the Nikon Z5 II

How far to stand from the subject with the Nikon Z5 II held vertically, so the subject fills 90% of the frame height. Full body means a 1.8 m person, head and shoulders about 0.5 m.

  • With 35 mm on the Nikon Z5 II: about 1.9 m (6.4 ft) for a full-body portrait, 0.5 m (1.8 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 50 mm on the Nikon Z5 II: about 2.8 m (9.1 ft) for a full-body portrait, 0.8 m (2.5 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 85 mm on the Nikon Z5 II: about 4.7 m (15 ft) for a full-body portrait, 1.3 m (4.3 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 135 mm on the Nikon Z5 II: about 7.5 m (25 ft) for a full-body portrait, 2.1 m (6.8 ft) for head and shoulders.

Questions

What does a 50 mm lens look like on the Nikon Z5 II?

It frames like 50 mm on full frame, with a horizontal angle of view of 39.6°.

Should I get a 35 mm or a 50 mm lens for the Nikon Z5 II?

On the Nikon Z5 II, 35 mm covers about 3.1 × 2.1 m at 3 m and 50 mm about 2.2 × 1.4 m. The wider 35 mm suits streets, travel and rooms; 50 mm is tighter and suits portraits and details. Compare both over a photo in the free lens comparison tool, or see them live through your iPhone with Prospekt.

How far do I stand for a full-body portrait with an 85 mm lens on the Nikon Z5 II?

About 4.7 m (15 ft) from the subject, with the camera held vertically. That fits a 1.8 m person in 90% of the frame height. For head and shoulders, stand about 1.3 m (4.3 ft) away.

How much does a 35 mm lens cover at 3 m on the Nikon Z5 II?

About 3.1 × 2.1 m, width by height, with the camera held horizontally. At 10 m it covers about 10.3 × 6.9 m.
