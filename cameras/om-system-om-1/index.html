OM System OM-1: what each focal length frames

The OM System OM-1 has a 2x crop factor (Micro Four Thirds sensor), so a lens frames like its focal length times 2x on full frame. A 50 mm lens on the OM System OM-1 frames like 100 mm on full frame.

Lens mount: Micro Four Thirds.

To see these focal lengths live, Prospekt has the OM System OM-1 built in: pick a focal length, point your iPhone, and the screen frames what that lens would on the OM System OM-1. iPhone app, $3.99 once, no subscription.

Classic full-frame looks on the OM System OM-1

To get the framing a full-frame shooter means by these focal lengths, use these on the OM System OM-1:

  • To frame like 24 mm on full frame, use 12 mm on the OM System OM-1.
  • To frame like 35 mm on full frame, use 18 mm on the OM System OM-1.
  • To frame like 50 mm on full frame, use 25 mm on the OM System OM-1.
  • To frame like 85 mm on full frame, use 42 mm on the OM System OM-1.
  • To frame like 135 mm on full frame, use 68 mm on the OM System OM-1.

Common zoom ranges on the OM System OM-1

  • A 12-40 mm zoom on the OM System OM-1 frames like 24-80 mm on full frame, a horizontal angle of view from 71.6° to 24.4°.
  • A 12-60 mm zoom on the OM System OM-1 frames like 24-120 mm on full frame, a horizontal angle of view from 71.6° to 16.4°.
  • A 14-42 mm zoom on the OM System OM-1 frames like 28-84 mm on full frame, a horizontal angle of view from 63.4° to 23.3°.
  • A 40-150 mm zoom on the OM System OM-1 frames like 80-300 mm on full frame, a horizontal angle of view from 24.4° to 6.6°.

Portrait distances on the OM System OM-1

How far to stand from the subject with the OM System OM-1 held vertically, so the subject fills 90% of the frame height. Full body means a 1.8 m person, head and shoulders about 0.5 m.

  • With 35 mm on the OM System OM-1: about 4.0 m (13 ft) for a full-body portrait, 1.1 m (3.7 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 50 mm on the OM System OM-1: about 5.8 m (19 ft) for a full-body portrait, 1.6 m (5.3 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 85 mm on the OM System OM-1: about 9.8 m (32 ft) for a full-body portrait, 2.7 m (9.0 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 135 mm on the OM System OM-1: about 15.6 m (51 ft) for a full-body portrait, 4.3 m (14 ft) for head and shoulders.

Questions

What does a 50 mm lens look like on the OM System OM-1?

It frames like 100 mm on full frame, with a horizontal angle of view of 19.6°.

Which lens gives a 50 mm full-frame look on the OM System OM-1?

A lens of about 25 mm. It frames like 50 mm on full frame.

Should I get a 35 mm or a 50 mm lens for the OM System OM-1?

On the OM System OM-1, 35 mm frames like 70 mm on full frame and covers about 1.5 × 1.1 m at 3 m; 50 mm frames like 100 mm and covers about 1.0 × 0.8 m. The wider 35 mm suits streets, travel and rooms; 50 mm is tighter and suits portraits and details. For the classic full-frame 35 mm look, use about 18 mm.

How far do I stand for a full-body portrait with an 85 mm lens on the OM System OM-1?

About 9.8 m (32 ft) from the subject, with the camera held vertically. That fits a 1.8 m person in 90% of the frame height. For head and shoulders, stand about 2.7 m (9.0 ft) away.

How much does a 35 mm lens cover at 3 m on the OM System OM-1?

About 1.5 × 1.1 m, width by height, with the camera held horizontally. At 10 m it covers about 4.9 × 3.7 m.
