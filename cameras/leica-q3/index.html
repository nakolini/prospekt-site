Leica Q3: what each focal length frames

The Leica Q3 has a full-frame sensor, so every lens frames exactly as marked. A 50 mm lens on the Leica Q3 frames like 50 mm, with a horizontal angle of view of 39.6°.

The Q3 has a fixed 28 mm lens, so you cannot change lenses. Its digital crop modes frame like 35, 50, 75 and 90 mm, and the table shows what those look like.
