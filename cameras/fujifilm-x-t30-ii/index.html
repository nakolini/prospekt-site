Fujifilm X-T30 II: what each focal length frames

The Fujifilm X-T30 II has a 1.5x crop factor (APS-C sensor), so a lens frames like its focal length times 1.5x on full frame. A 50 mm lens on the Fujifilm X-T30 II frames like 75 mm on full frame.

Lens mount: Fujifilm X.

To see these focal lengths live, Prospekt has the Fujifilm X-T30 II built in: pick a focal length, point your iPhone, and the screen frames what that lens would on the Fujifilm X-T30 II. iPhone app, $3.99 once, no subscription.

Classic full-frame looks on the Fujifilm X-T30 II

To get the framing a full-frame shooter means by these focal lengths, use these on the Fujifilm X-T30 II:

  • To frame like 24 mm on full frame, use 16 mm on the Fujifilm X-T30 II.
  • To frame like 35 mm on full frame, use 23 mm on the Fujifilm X-T30 II.
  • To frame like 50 mm on full frame, use 33 mm on the Fujifilm X-T30 II.
  • To frame like 85 mm on full frame, use 57 mm on the Fujifilm X-T30 II.
  • To frame like 135 mm on full frame, use 90 mm on the Fujifilm X-T30 II.

Portrait distances on the Fujifilm X-T30 II

How far to stand from the subject with the Fujifilm X-T30 II held vertically, so the subject fills 90% of the frame height. Full body means a 1.8 m person, head and shoulders about 0.5 m.

  • With 35 mm on the Fujifilm X-T30 II: about 2.9 m (9.6 ft) for a full-body portrait, 0.8 m (2.7 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 50 mm on the Fujifilm X-T30 II: about 4.2 m (14 ft) for a full-body portrait, 1.2 m (3.8 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 85 mm on the Fujifilm X-T30 II: about 7.1 m (23 ft) for a full-body portrait, 2.0 m (6.5 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 135 mm on the Fujifilm X-T30 II: about 11.2 m (37 ft) for a full-body portrait, 3.1 m (10 ft) for head and shoulders.

Questions

What does a 50 mm lens look like on the Fujifilm X-T30 II?

It frames like 75 mm on full frame, with a horizontal angle of view of 27.0°.

Which lens gives a 50 mm full-frame look on the Fujifilm X-T30 II?

A lens of about 33 mm. It frames like 50 mm on full frame.

Should I get a 35 mm or a 50 mm lens for the Fujifilm X-T30 II?

On the Fujifilm X-T30 II, 35 mm frames like 52 mm on full frame and covers about 2.1 × 1.4 m at 3 m; 50 mm frames like 75 mm and covers about 1.4 × 1.0 m. The wider 35 mm suits streets, travel and rooms; 50 mm is tighter and suits portraits and details. For the classic full-frame 35 mm look, use about 23 mm.

How far do I stand for a full-body portrait with an 85 mm lens on the Fujifilm X-T30 II?

About 7.1 m (23 ft) from the subject, with the camera held vertically. That fits a 1.8 m person in 90% of the frame height. For head and shoulders, stand about 2.0 m (6.5 ft) away.

How much does a 35 mm lens cover at 3 m on the Fujifilm X-T30 II?

About 2.1 × 1.4 m, width by height, with the camera held horizontally. At 10 m it covers about 6.9 × 4.6 m.
