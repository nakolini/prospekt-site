OM System OM-5: what each focal length frames

The OM System OM-5 has a 2x crop factor (Micro Four Thirds sensor), so a lens frames like its focal length times 2x on full frame. A 50 mm lens on the OM System OM-5 frames like 100 mm on full frame.

Lens mount: Micro Four Thirds.

Classic full-frame looks on the OM System OM-5

To get the framing a full-frame shooter means by these focal lengths, use these on the OM System OM-5:

  • To frame like 24 mm on full frame, use 12 mm on the OM System OM-5.
  • To frame like 35 mm on full frame, use 18 mm on the OM System OM-5.
  • To frame like 50 mm on full frame, use 25 mm on the OM System OM-5.
  • To frame like 85 mm on full frame, use 42 mm on the OM System OM-5.
  • To frame like 135 mm on full frame, use 68 mm on the OM System OM-5.

Questions

What does a 50 mm lens look like on the OM System OM-5?

It frames like 100 mm on full frame, with a horizontal angle of view of 19.6°.

Which lens gives a 50 mm full-frame look on the OM System OM-5?

A lens of about 25 mm. It frames like 50 mm on full frame.
