Hasselblad 907X 上的经典全画幅焦段

要在 Hasselblad 907X 上得到全画幅用户所说的这些焦距的取景，请使用：

  • 要得到全画幅 24 mm 的取景，在 Hasselblad 907X 上用 30 mm。
  • 要得到全画幅 35 mm 的取景，在 Hasselblad 907X 上用 44 mm。
  • 要得到全画幅 50 mm 的取景，在 Hasselblad 907X 上用 63 mm。
  • 要得到全画幅 85 mm 的取景，在 Hasselblad 907X 上用 108 mm。
  • 要得到全画幅 135 mm 的取景，在 Hasselblad 907X 上用 171 mm。

Portrait distances on the Hasselblad 907X

How far to stand from the subject with the Hasselblad 907X held vertically, so the subject fills 90% of the frame height. Full body means a 1.8 m person, head and shoulders about 0.5 m.

  • With 35 mm on the Hasselblad 907X: about 1.6 m (5.2 ft) for a full-body portrait, 0.4 m (1.5 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 50 mm on the Hasselblad 907X: about 2.3 m (7.5 ft) for a full-body portrait, 0.6 m (2.1 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 85 mm on the Hasselblad 907X: about 3.9 m (13 ft) for a full-body portrait, 1.1 m (3.5 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 135 mm on the Hasselblad 907X: about 6.2 m (20 ft) for a full-body portrait, 1.7 m (5.6 ft) for head and shoulders.

常见问题

50 mm 镜头在 Hasselblad 907X 上是什么效果？

取景相当于全画幅的 40 mm，水平视角为 47.3°。

在 Hasselblad 907X 上用哪支镜头能得到全画幅 50 mm 的效果？

大约 63 mm 的镜头。它的取景相当于全画幅的 50 mm。

How far do I stand for a full-body portrait with an 85 mm lens on the Hasselblad 907X?

About 3.9 m (13 ft) from the subject, with the camera held vertically. That fits a 1.8 m person in 90% of the frame height. For head and shoulders, stand about 1.1 m (3.5 ft) away.

How much does a 35 mm lens cover at 3 m on the Hasselblad 907X?

About 3.8 × 2.8 m, width by height, with the camera held horizontally. At 10 m it covers about 12.5 × 9.4 m.
