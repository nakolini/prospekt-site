Fujifilm X-S20 上的经典全画幅焦段

要在 Fujifilm X-S20 上得到全画幅用户所说的这些焦距的取景，请使用：

  • 要得到全画幅 24 mm 的取景，在 Fujifilm X-S20 上用 16 mm。
  • 要得到全画幅 35 mm 的取景，在 Fujifilm X-S20 上用 23 mm。
  • 要得到全画幅 50 mm 的取景，在 Fujifilm X-S20 上用 33 mm。
  • 要得到全画幅 85 mm 的取景，在 Fujifilm X-S20 上用 57 mm。
  • 要得到全画幅 135 mm 的取景，在 Fujifilm X-S20 上用 90 mm。

Portrait distances on the Fujifilm X-S20

How far to stand from the subject with the Fujifilm X-S20 held vertically, so the subject fills 90% of the frame height. Full body means a 1.8 m person, head and shoulders about 0.5 m.

  • With 35 mm on the Fujifilm X-S20: about 2.9 m (9.6 ft) for a full-body portrait, 0.8 m (2.7 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 50 mm on the Fujifilm X-S20: about 4.2 m (14 ft) for a full-body portrait, 1.2 m (3.8 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 85 mm on the Fujifilm X-S20: about 7.1 m (23 ft) for a full-body portrait, 2.0 m (6.5 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 135 mm on the Fujifilm X-S20: about 11.2 m (37 ft) for a full-body portrait, 3.1 m (10 ft) for head and shoulders.

常见问题

50 mm 镜头在 Fujifilm X-S20 上是什么效果？

取景相当于全画幅的 75 mm，水平视角为 27.0°。

在 Fujifilm X-S20 上用哪支镜头能得到全画幅 50 mm 的效果？

大约 33 mm 的镜头。它的取景相当于全画幅的 50 mm。

How far do I stand for a full-body portrait with an 85 mm lens on the Fujifilm X-S20?

About 7.1 m (23 ft) from the subject, with the camera held vertically. That fits a 1.8 m person in 90% of the frame height. For head and shoulders, stand about 2.0 m (6.5 ft) away.

How much does a 35 mm lens cover at 3 m on the Fujifilm X-S20?

About 2.1 × 1.4 m, width by height, with the camera held horizontally. At 10 m it covers about 6.9 × 4.6 m.
