Hasselblad X1D II: cosa inquadra ogni lunghezza focale

La Hasselblad X1D II ha un fattore di crop di 0,79x (sensore Medio formato 44 x 33), quindi un obiettivo inquadra come la sua lunghezza focale moltiplicata per 0,79x su full frame. Un obiettivo da 50 mm sulla Hasselblad X1D II inquadra come un 40 mm su full frame.

Innesto: Hasselblad XCD.

  • Per inquadrare come un 24 mm su full frame, usa un 30 mm sulla Hasselblad X1D II.
  • Per inquadrare come un 35 mm su full frame, usa un 44 mm sulla Hasselblad X1D II.
  • Per inquadrare come un 50 mm su full frame, usa un 63 mm sulla Hasselblad X1D II.

Portrait distances on the Hasselblad X1D II

How far to stand from the subject with the Hasselblad X1D II held vertically, so the subject fills 90% of the frame height. Full body means a 1.8 m person, head and shoulders about 0.5 m.

  • With 35 mm on the Hasselblad X1D II: about 1,6 m (5,2 ft) for a full-body portrait, 0,4 m (1,5 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 50 mm on the Hasselblad X1D II: about 2,3 m (7,5 ft) for a full-body portrait, 0,6 m (2,1 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 85 mm on the Hasselblad X1D II: about 3,9 m (13 ft) for a full-body portrait, 1,1 m (3,5 ft) for head and shoulders.
  • With 135 mm on the Hasselblad X1D II: about 6,2 m (20 ft) for a full-body portrait, 1,7 m (5,6 ft) for head and shoulders.

Domande

Come appare un obiettivo da 50 mm sulla Hasselblad X1D II?

Inquadra come un 40 mm su full frame, con un angolo di campo orizzontale di 47,3°.

Quale obiettivo dà l'inquadratura di un 50 mm full frame sulla Hasselblad X1D II?

Un obiettivo di circa 63 mm. Inquadra come un 50 mm su full frame.

How far do I stand for a full-body portrait with an 85 mm lens on the Hasselblad X1D II?

About 3,9 m (13 ft) from the subject, with the camera held vertically. That fits a 1.8 m person in 90% of the frame height. For head and shoulders, stand about 1,1 m (3,5 ft) away.

How much does a 35 mm lens cover at 3 m on the Hasselblad X1D II?

About 3,8 × 2,8 m, width by height, with the camera held horizontally. At 10 m it covers about 12,5 × 9,4 m.
